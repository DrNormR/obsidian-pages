# Running

![Pasted image 20260915193129.png|393](/img/user/NCAA-Football/College%20Football%2027/~media/Pasted%20image%2020260915193129.png)
- do not run in direction of the flame icons--those are unblocked
- as soon as you snap, identify the hole and use left stick only, no turbo
	- snap left stick back and forth
- if defense is in the backfield quickly, hold turbo and bounce run outside

## Option
- read option
	- identify the R player to read
	- If he crashes down, hold X
	- If he stays home, stay off of X
- RPO
	- RPO bubble out of trips
	- RPO flat
- speed option
	- identify the P pitch guy
	- pitch with L1
	- not recommended
